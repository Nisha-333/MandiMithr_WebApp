# 🌾 MandiMithr

**MandiMithr** is a decision-support platform that helps farmers and agri-operators discover the **most profitable mandis**, not just the highest market rates.  
It combines **real-time price data**, **transport costs**, and **mandi commission** to calculate **net profit**, enabling smarter selling decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔎 Frictionless Mandi Finder
- Simple inputs: **crop**, **PIN code**, and **quantity**
- Finds the best nearby mandis within a configurable radius (e.g. **50–150 km**)

### 💰 Profit Calculator
- Net profit computation using:
  - Market price
  - Quantity
  - Transport distance
  - Mandi commission
- Clear and transparent cost breakdown

### 🗺️ Map-Based View
- Visualize farmer location and nearby mandis
- Color-coded markers:
  - 🟢 Best
  - 🟡 Decent
  - 🔴 Avoid

### 📈 Price Trends
- Short-term price history
- Simple signals: **rising / steady / falling**
- Helps decide **when to sell**

### 📚 Economics Education Hub
- Built-in explainers for:
  - MSP (Minimum Support Price)
  - Commission structures
  - Demand cycles
  - When *not* to sell

### 📊 Comparative Market View
- Side-by-side comparison of mandis
- Sort by:
  - Price
  - Distance
  - Net profit
